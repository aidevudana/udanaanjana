About Me

Medicine and AI,
one discipline

I'm Udana Anjana, a final-year medical undergraduate at the Faculty of Medicine and Allied Sciences, Rajarata University of Sri Lanka, with a clinical interest that runs from bedside medicine to aerospace medicine — how the body behaves at altitude, under acceleration, and in environments it was never built for.

Alongside my studies I've founded three ventures: Doctor WEB, the healthcare platform behind Bestdoctor.lk; Studio Dark Arts, a photography and film studio; and Med Insight Media. I'm also tech lead at the Rajarata Gynecological Endoscopy Center. On the technical side I build the whole stack around a model — training and evaluation, the web and mobile app in front of it, the cloud underneath.

As a photographer and DOP I shoot portraits, real estate, music video and event work, and I run the marketing that surrounds it: tourism and hospitality campaigns, social media, SEO, web development and brand strategy. A radiologist reading a scan and a detection model scoring the same image are doing the same job with different instruments; framing a shot is that discipline again. Medicine is what makes the rest useful — I know where a model's errors actually land on a patient, which is the part most engineering teams are missing.
